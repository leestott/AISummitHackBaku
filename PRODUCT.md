# Caspian Compass

## Register

product

## Users

City-break travellers, families, regional explorers, conference attendees adding
leisure time, and presenters who need a repeatable Azerbaijan travel demo.

## Product purpose

Turn dates, interests, origin, and budget into a useful Baku and Azerbaijan trip
plan, then demonstrate a clearly fictional booking without contacting suppliers
or accepting real payments.

## Brand personality

Grounded, welcoming, and clear. Use the specification's Caspian deep teal,
sandstone accents, and generous whitespace. Travellers use the interface on a
laptop in daylight while comparing plans, or on a phone while checking transit;
a light, legible interface and strong controls serve both.

## Anti-references

Never resemble a deceptive live booking site: no false urgency, invented
verification claims, hidden synthetic labels, or confirmations resembling real
tickets. Avoid an AI-chat shell that hides the structured trip workflow.

## Design principles

1. Make the next planning decision obvious.
2. Keep prices, source dates, and uncertainty beside the relevant information.
3. Keep the simulation boundary visible throughout the journey.
4. Make structured travel planning useful even when the hosted agent is offline.
5. Use progressive detail rather than dense configuration screens.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, accessible
tabs, live search feedback, reduced motion, and keyboard alternatives to dragging.
Provide currency formatting for AZN, GBP, USD, and EUR, with a dated demo rate.
Prepare interface copy for English, Azerbaijani, and Russian localization.
